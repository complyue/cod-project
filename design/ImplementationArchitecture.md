### Registering and importing an empty procedure family in the seed

When `register_module` validates a local procedure symbol, establish one program-owned `CheckedFamily` with that nominal symbol and declaring module. Reuse the same registration operation for subsequent admissible headers and methods. Keep the symbol’s declared procedure type, any callable signature declarations, and the executable methods distinct. Publish the family index even when it contains no methods. Apply the existing classification for procedure symbols, ordinary functions, reference/value symbols and intrinsic services. Check applicability and coverage wherever a lexical call or named installation demands an implementation. Dynamic calls continue to follow the actual installation. [Declaration contract](SymbolicDynamicScope.md#procedure-families-before-the-first-method)

Use the same resource binder for explicit, default-expanded and complete selections. Validate the public kind, the receiving spelling and any pending local declaration before recording the selection. A procedure import introduces a free name or reuses the same identity and selects the receiver’s interpretation. Repeated equivalent imports share that receiver family, including its declarations and later contributions. An identity-only directive before or after the import contributes its symbol binding and preserves the import. Extension through a local spelling requires the grant for that spelling. Importing under one name cannot authorize a contribution under another name selected only by `using` or `symbol from`. Keep source-preserving views and distinct receiving interpretations distinguishable, with a located stop for an unproved composition. [Selection contract](ModuleWorlds.md#selecting-a-symbolic-procedure-family)

Reuse the program’s family indices for publication, adaptation and deduplication. `CheckedProgram::families` and `methods` are deques. Appending at the end preserves references to existing elements. The imported family owns its declaration and method lists, with the source origin retained. Family registration and selection require no TLS installation, generated-code changes or native ABI.

The runner’s import fixture may use a dedicated provider which explicitly imports the empty family, defines and exports its first method, and emits the module-initialization observation once. The receiver imports the populated family under a local name, selects the same variable symbol under a local name, and explicitly installs the handler after setting the variable to 7. After a write of 8, `perform` must observe 7 and `behave` must observe 8. Check the full output `1001/907/908/8` and the runner’s LLVM IR and bitcode artifacts. Use distinct source and receiver evidence to demonstrate method interpretation. Pending tests that require body instantiation may be removed from the bootstrap suites. Keep the ordinary import workload and independently supported selector and diagnostic checks. Future body-template acceptance belongs with the deferred implementation.

Verify both seed modes on an ordinary bodyless header, an empty procedure symbol, an authorized first method and an independent source-preserving selection. Cover the optional identity directive in both orders, repeated imports, source and receiver isolation, wrong kind, same-signature nominal conflict, pending local declarations, and extension attempted through a second spelling without a grant. Check an empty-family call and named installation, plus a dynamic call under an independently installed compatible handler. Replay the affected seed inputs. The linked CoD booter importer has a separate review and delivery scope.

## Design review and delivery responsibilities

The design discussion interprets confirmed semantics, reviews the structure of each proposed code batch, and publishes the scoped design decision and acceptance criteria. It can examine source, diffs and existing verification records to assess a claim. When a change would weaken a confirmed guarantee or requires a material choice outside its delegated scope, it takes that choice to the project user.

Development mainline owns the implementation from correction through production cutover. It identifies the affected commands, establishes what the previous version accepted, runs the compatibility checks against the exact candidate, fixes regressions and publishes the first checkpoint that preserves the required behavior. Mainline makes that delivery decision within the cleared scope. Fresh design review applies when the proposed architecture or contract changes.

For example, if the user asks whether a redesigned AST is in production, the design discussion can inspect the public commit and ask what the production driver still needs. If the supplied results cover object emission but omit provider generation, the reviewer identifies the missing comparison and asks mainline for its result. Mainline owns the comparison, repair and implementation push. The reviewer reports the outcome or the outstanding blocker to the user.

## Before coding

Start with the intended idiomatic source and the real acceptance workload. Include the smallest coherent seed increment needed to express that source under confirmed language contracts. Use the intended source for delivery and keep temporary substitutes isolated. Send the proposed structure to the CoD design discussion and obtain explicit clearance for the identified batch before writing new code. Include tests, scripts, native glue, tooling and new code inside existing files. The design discussion settles questions within the user's principles and brings material trade-offs to the user.
